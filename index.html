About me

I am a civil engineer specialising in highway and transportation engineering, working where field data, design standards and road safety meet.

As Senior Project Engineer with the Research and Consultancy Cell of the Transportation Planning and Engineering Section at SVNIT, Surat, I lead and coordinate consultancy projects for government agencies and urban bodies: from traffic surveys and field investigations to technical reports, estimates and DPRs.

My research focuses on how highway geometry and vehicle behaviour affect operating speeds and crash risk on multi-lane highways. I want to produce research that can be applied directly in the field, and to bring better design approaches into everyday practice.

Research

My current area of research and how it is carried out.

Area of research

Reliability-based road safety evaluation: a study of multi-lane highways in plain terrain

A framework combining field data, statistical modelling and reliability analysis to measure how geometric design and vehicle characteristics affect operating speeds and crash severity. The resulting alignment-based safety indicators support evidence-based improvements on multi-lane national highways.

  1. Data collection

    Radar gun speed surveys, plus geometric and crash records.

  2. Speed prediction

    Linear regression models for operating speed.

  3. Design consistency

    First-Order Reliability Method (FORM) for safety reliability.

  4. Crash modelling

    Zero/one-inflated beta regression and a Poisson-Tweedie model.

  5. Alignment indices

    CCR and CRR to link geometric variability with crashes.
