About

I'm a civil engineer specialising in highway and transportation engineering, working where field data, design standards and road safety meet.

I work as a Senior Project Engineer with the Research and Consultancy Cell of the Transportation Planning and Engineering Section at SVNIT, Surat, where I lead and coordinate transportation planning, traffic engineering and road safety consultancy projects, from field investigations to final reports and DPRs.

My research looks at how highway geometry and vehicle behaviour shape operating speeds and crash risk. I want to produce research that engineers can actually apply in the field, and to work with teams bringing better design methods and technology into practice.

Research

Current area of research

Reliability-based road safety evaluation: a study of multi-lane highways in plain terrain

A framework that combines field data, statistical modelling and reliability analysis to measure how geometric design and vehicle characteristics affect operating speeds and crash severity. The resulting alignment-based safety indicators support evidence-based improvements on multi-lane national highways.

  1. Data collection

    Radar gun speed surveys, plus geometric and crash records.

  2. Speed prediction

    Linear regression models for operating speed.

  3. Design consistency

    First-Order Reliability Method (FORM) to quantify safety reliability.

  4. Crash modelling

    Zero/one-inflated beta regression and a Poisson-Tweedie frequency model.

  5. Alignment indices

    CCR and CRR to link geometric variability with crashes.
